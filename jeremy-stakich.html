Jeremy Stakich

Creator of Stress Free Flow

Jeremy Stakich is the creator of Stress Free Flow, a calm app for iPhone and iPad. He designed and built it himself, from the first screen to the App Store listing. It started as something for Ollie, his non-verbal great-nephew, and grew into an app for families, caregivers, and anyone who needs a quiet reset.

He has spent 20 years building and supporting software, with a bachelor’s in business and an associate’s in technology. Stress Free Flow is free to download. Pro is a one-time $4.99 unlock. There is no subscription and no ads.
