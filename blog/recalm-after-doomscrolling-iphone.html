How to Recalm After Doomscrolling on iPhone

September 2, 2026 · Anxiety · Self regulation · iPhone

To recalm after doomscrolling, first end the stream of new information. You do not need to prove that every concern is unfounded or become instantly relaxed. The immediate job is to move from an endless feed to the present room, identify whether any real action is required, and give attention a finite place to land.

Exit without one last refresh

Close the feed at the current item. Put the phone down and name the date, time, and your location. Notice three stable things around you, then feel the support under your body. If a story raised a concrete concern, write its topic—not the whole argument—in Notes for a scheduled check later.

Use a 3-2-1 recalm sequence

  • Three: identify three neutral sights or sounds in your actual environment.
  • Two: check two body needs, such as water, food, warmth, movement, or rest.
  • One: choose one bounded next action: make tea, shower, call someone, or verify one fact from a primary source.

For prevention settings and feed exits, use how to stop doomscrolling when anxious.

Decide what informed means for today

Being informed does not require continuous exposure. Choose one or two reliable sources, a check-in time, and a stopping point. Turn off breaking-news alerts that do not affect immediate safety. If an event does require action, define it clearly—donate, contact someone, prepare supplies, or follow local instructions—then leave the feed.

FAQ

Is doomscrolling always about bad news?

No. Any repetitive feed checking driven by uncertainty or distress can create the same open-ended attention loop.

Should I avoid the news completely?

Not necessarily. A scheduled, source-limited check can keep you informed while protecting the rest of the day.

What if the story affects my immediate safety?

Follow official local guidance and contact emergency services when appropriate. A calming exercise should not delay necessary action.
