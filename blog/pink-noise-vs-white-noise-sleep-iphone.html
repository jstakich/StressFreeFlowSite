Pink Noise vs White Noise for Sleep on iPhone

By Jeremy Stakich · September 4, 2026 · Sleep · Sounds · iPhone

The pink noise vs white noise sleep choice is mostly about which spectrum sounds comfortable and masks your particular environment at a low volume. White noise has equal energy per hertz and often sounds brighter or hissier. Pink noise reduces energy as frequency rises, which many listeners describe as softer or more balanced. Neither is guaranteed to improve sleep.

How pink and white noise differ

White noise distributes power evenly across equal-width frequency bands, so high frequencies are prominent to the ear. Pink noise distributes power evenly across octaves, producing relatively more low-frequency energy. Speakers and recordings also color the result, so labels are not a substitute for listening. For another comparison, see brown noise vs pink noise.

Match the sound to the disturbance

  • Try white noise for sharper, higher-frequency household sounds if its hiss is comfortable.
  • Try pink noise when white noise feels too bright or when you prefer a softer wash.
  • Place the phone or speaker away from the pillow rather than increasing volume at close range.
  • Keep alarms, smoke detectors, children, and other important cues audible.

Run a simple three-night test

Use the same bedtime window and device position for three nights with pink, then three with white, keeping volume low and similar. In the morning, note comfort, awakenings you remember, and whether the sound itself became distracting. Do not overinterpret one unusual night. If both annoy you, silence, a fan, rain, or another stable sound may fit better. Apple also documents built-in Background Sounds on supported devices.

FAQ

Is pink noise scientifically proven to improve sleep?

Research is still limited and results vary. Treat noise as an optional masking preference, not a proven treatment.

Which noise should be louder?

Neither. Start quietly and use only enough level to soften the disturbance while keeping important sounds audible.

Can I play noise all night from iPhone?

Many apps and Apple features support extended playback. Confirm power, Lock Screen behavior, alarms, and comfortable volume before relying on a setup.
