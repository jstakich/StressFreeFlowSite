## Blog usage notes

- Title/subtitle terms (**Mental Reset**, **Overstimulation**, **Sensory Tool**) are highest App Store weight — blog support helps Google + reinforces listing language.
- Phrase splits in the keyword field (`chill,out`, `quiet,rest`, `calm,break`, etc.) can become natural H1/primary keywords.
- Check `/blog` and `blogs.json` before targeting a term so posts do not cannibalize.
- Already-strong coverage examples: Breath Reset, Sensory Overload, Overstimulation (phone), Mental Reset, Calm Break, Mood Reset, Quiet Rest / chill out — prefer fresh angles or secondary terms when those are saturated.
